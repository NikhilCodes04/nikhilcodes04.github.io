About

I'm a backend-leaning full-stack engineer. Most of my time goes into the parts users never see: schema design, query plans, service boundaries.

Day to day that means distributed systems work: replacing pessimistic locking with PostgreSQL sequences, rebuilding a ClickHouse query engine around an AST model, and wiring policy-driven authorization across microservices over gRPC.

The other half is AI. I've built LLM orchestration pipelines at Samsung PRISM, the first LLM-powered system at my university's Data Science & AI lab, and a multilingual voice assistant on OpenAI APIs with speech-to-text and TTS.

What interests me there is the plumbing that makes model output dependable: prompt structure, parsing, fallbacks, session state. Correctness and latency both matter.
